Collection scope

Per symbol

  • Bybit USDT linear perpetual metadata: launch time, funding interval, caps/floors, min qty/notional, max leverage.
  • 1h candles for the selected lookback window.
  • Funding history for the same window.
  • 1h open interest history.
  • 1h account long/short ratio.

Derived fields

  • Returns over 24h, 3d, 7d, and 14d.
  • Drawdown from lookback high and high from first close.
  • Funding sums over 24h, 3d, and 7d.
  • OI change over 4h and 24h.
  • Preliminary pump score, continuation risk score, and candidate tier.

These tiers are research labels only. They are not live entry or exit criteria.
